# Code of Conduct

Be decent to people. Treat everyone with respect, whether you agree with them or not. This project is a welcoming place for anyone who wants to help, regardless of who they are or where they come from.

## What we expect

- Be kind, patient, and respectful in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Give and take feedback gracefully. Critique the code, not the person.
- Assume good intent. Most misunderstandings are just that.
- Help newcomers. Everyone was new once.

## What is not okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about who someone is.
- Unwelcome personal attention, trolling, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Sharing someone's private information without their clear permission.

This applies everywhere the project lives: issues, pull requests, comments, and any other project space, in public or in private.

## If something goes wrong

If you see or experience behavior that breaks this Code of Conduct, report it privately:

- Email: yashwanthreddykatipally@gmail.com

Reports are taken seriously and handled confidentially. The maintainer will review what happened and respond in a way that fits, which may include a warning, removing comments, or blocking someone from the project. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face the same consequences.
