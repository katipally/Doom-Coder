# DoomCoder Companion — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 2026**

## Summary

DoomCoder Companion does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All synchronisation happens privately through your own iCloud account using Apple CloudKit.

## What data the app uses

Your prompts and notes are stored **on your device**. If you pair a Mac, DoomCoder Companion syncs the following data **solely between your own devices** via your private iCloud account:

- The list of AI coding agents configured in DoomCoder on your Mac
- Notification logs generated by those agents
- Mac status/availability information

This data is stored in your **private CloudKit database** — it is never accessible to us or any third party. Apple's CloudKit privacy guarantees apply.

## What data we collect

**None.** We do not operate any servers, analytics services, or accounts. We do not collect crash reports, usage statistics, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information.

## AI features (optional)

The optional **Enhance** feature can improve a prompt using AI, in a mode you choose:

- **On-device (Apple Intelligence)** — runs entirely on your device; no prompt text leaves it.
- **My API key (BYOK)** — if you provide your own third-party provider key, only the specific text you choose to enhance is sent to that provider you selected, subject to its own privacy policy. We do not operate or proxy this request and receive none of it. Your key is stored only in this device's Keychain.

Prompts and notes never leave your device unless you explicitly use the BYOK Enhance option on a given piece of text.

## Push Notifications

Notifications are delivered via Apple Push Notification service (APNs) using CloudKit subscriptions, and local note reminders via UserNotifications. Mac-originated notification content is routed through Apple's infrastructure. We never see the content of your notifications.

## Third-party services

If you opt in to BYOK Enhance, requests go directly to the AI provider whose key you supplied. Otherwise the app uses only Apple frameworks (CloudKit, UserNotifications, FoundationModels, SwiftUI).
