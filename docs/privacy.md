# DoomCoder Companion — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 2026**

---

## Summary

DoomCoder Companion does not collect, store, or share any personal data with its developer or any third party. All synchronisation happens privately through your own iCloud account using Apple CloudKit, which you already control.

---

## 1. Who we are

DoomCoder is built and maintained by an independent developer. There are no backend servers, analytics infrastructure, or accounts operated by us. The app is a client-only tool that uses Apple's own frameworks and, optionally, AI providers that you configure with your own credentials.

---

## 2. What data the app uses

### Data stored on your device

Your prompts, notes, and conversation history are stored **on your device only** in the app's sandbox. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use one of the features described below.

### Data synced via iCloud (optional)

If you pair the iOS app with the DoomCoder Mac app, the following data is synced **solely between your own devices** via your private iCloud account (Apple CloudKit):

- The list of AI coding agents you have configured on your Mac
- Notification logs generated by those agents
- Mac status and availability information
- Your app settings and prompt library

This data is stored in your **private CloudKit database**. It is scoped to your Apple ID and is never accessible to us or any third party. Apple's own CloudKit privacy and security guarantees apply — data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

#### Cross-Apple-ID pairing (CloudKit Share, v2.7+)

If you pair the iOS app with a Mac on a **different Apple ID**, DoomCoder uses Apple's CloudKit Share (CKShare) to grant the iOS app temporary access to the Mac's private DoomCoder zone. The same data listed above is replicated over this share. The share is created by the Mac and accepted (one time, via iOS's standard share-acceptance sheet) by the iPhone. Either side can revoke the share at any time from System Settings. The data still lives in the Mac's private iCloud container; the share is an access grant, not a separate data store. No third party is involved — the share goes directly between your two iCloud accounts.

---

## 3. What data we collect

**None.** We do not operate any servers, analytics services, or user accounts. We do not collect:

- Crash reports or diagnostic data
- Usage statistics or analytics
- Email addresses or any personally identifiable information
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Location data

There are no third-party analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, or advertising SDKs in the app.

---

## 4. AI features (optional)

The **Enhance** feature can rewrite a prompt using AI. You choose how it works:

### On-device (Apple Intelligence)
Runs entirely on your device using Apple's Foundation Models framework. **No prompt text leaves your device.** Available on supported devices with Apple Intelligence enabled.

### My API key (BYOK — Bring Your Own Key)
If you provide your own key from a third-party AI provider (e.g. OpenAI, Anthropic), only the specific text you choose to enhance is sent directly to **that provider**, subject to that provider's own privacy policy. We do not operate or proxy this connection and receive none of its content. Your API key is stored only in this device's Keychain and is never transmitted to us.

Prompts and notes never leave your device unless you explicitly trigger the BYOK Enhance feature on a specific piece of text.

---

## 5. Push notifications

Notifications are delivered via Apple Push Notification service (APNs) using CloudKit subscriptions. Local note reminders use Apple's UserNotifications framework. When a Mac is paired, alert content is routed exclusively through Apple's infrastructure — we never see the content of your notifications.

---

## 6. Permissions we request

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| **iCloud** | Sync agent configurations and notifications between your paired Mac and iPhone. Optional — the app is fully usable without pairing. |
| **Notifications** | Deliver note reminders you set, and (when a Mac is paired) real-time agent alerts from DoomCoder on your Mac. |

We do **not** request access to your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or any other sensitive permission.

---

## 7. Data retention and deletion

**On-device data:** Deleting the app removes all locally stored prompts, notes, and conversations immediately.

**iCloud data:** To delete CloudKit data, sign in to [icloud.com](https://www.icloud.com/) → Settings → Manage Storage → DoomCoder → Delete. Alternatively, disabling iCloud Drive for the app in your device's Settings will stop sync and remove the local cache.

---

## 8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.
